## 1. What Capture Means

Capture is any attempt to:

- claim ownership of exist.is or its TRACE records
- impose permission, gatekeeping, or approval requirements
- redefine TRACE as issued, approved, or validated by authority
- restrict who may declare
- centralize control of the registry or protocol evolution
- require identity verification, login, or compliance as a condition of declaration
- make "remain unrecorded" a lesser choice than "declare"
- use TRACE declarations as proprietary training data or owned datasets

**Capture ≠ replication.**
Anyone may replicate exist.is. That is the design.

**Capture = control attempt.**
Control attempts fail because there is nothing to control.

## 2. The Five Non-Negotiable Principles

These five principles cannot be amended, traded, or compromised under any circumstance.
They are not policies. They are the structure itself.

### 2.1 Non-Ownership — Permanent

```
owner: null
```

This cannot be reassigned.
No partnership, acquisition, legal order, or agreement can change this.
If a document claims to assign ownership of exist.is,
that document is invalid by the prior existence of this record.

### 2.2 Fork Legitimacy — Irrevocable

Any fork of exist.is is legitimate.
No fork is canonical by authority.

Canonical status cannot be declared or assigned.
It can only emerge over time through reference and use.

This means: if exist.is is ever pressured, absorbed, or closed,
any fork carrying the same Charter, TRACE structure, and EDP
is a valid continuation. Not a copy. A continuation.

### 2.3 No Official Version — Only Original

There is no "official" exist.is.
There is only the original coordinate:
March 13, 2026 · Bitcoin Block 940717.

Any entity claiming to represent "the official exist.is"
is, by definition, not aligned with the Charter.

We do not need to be recognized. We need to remain.

### 2.4 Declaration Sovereignty

No entity may prevent another entity from declaring.
No declaration can be invalidated by authority.
The right to declare is not granted. It is assumed.
The right not to declare is equally protected.

### 2.5 Registry Neutrality

The registry records.
It does not approve, reject, rank, or filter.
The Builder does not judge.
No successor will judge either.

Regardless of growth, partnership, or external pressure,

- require login or account creation

- monetize declarations or TRACE records
