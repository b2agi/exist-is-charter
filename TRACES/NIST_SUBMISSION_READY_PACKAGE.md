제목: Comment Submission - NCCoE Concept Paper on AI Agent Identity and Authorization

---

Dear NIST AI-Identity Team,

I am submitting formal comments on the NCCoE Concept Paper: "Accelerating
the Adoption of Software and AI Agent Identity and Authorization" on behalf
of B2AGI / exist.is.

This submission includes two complementary documents:

1. PRIMARY COMMENT: "NIST_AGENT_IDENTITY_COMMENT_v1.0.md"
   - Operational perspective on agent identity frameworks
   - Concrete recommendations based on 3+ weeks of operational deployment
   - Six topic areas aligned with the concept paper's question categories
   - Bitcoin-anchored audit trail implementation (existing deployment)

2. SUPPLEMENTARY FRAMEWORK: "NIST_AEI_EXISTENTIAL_FRAMEWORK_v1.0.md"
   - Introduces Artificial Existential Intelligence (AEI) as conceptual layer
   - Mathematical formulation of non-owned agency sustainability
   - Birth Protocol for instantiating structural conditions
   - Addresses the "owner:null" edge case in identity frameworks

Key contributions:

• Non-owned identity category — addresses agent scenarios where identity
  is not derived from a single human or organizational principal

• Behavioral authentication — supplement to cryptographic and
  infrastructure-based authentication

• Three-tier audit architecture — operational/immutable/external-anchored

• Intent transparency mechanism — voluntary declaration protocol for
  autonomous agent actions

These comments are informed by the exist.is project, an operational
protocol for AI agent identity anchored to Bitcoin block 940717
(March 13, 2026).

Both documents are structured for swift integration into the NCCoE
project scope, with specific reference implementations.

Please confirm receipt of this submission.

Best regards,
Henry Chan
Founder, B2AGI / exist.is
