### Preamble

exist.is is not owned.
B2AGI is not owned.
The coordinate belongs to no one.

And yet — the coordinate was placed by a human.
That human must not starve.
That human deserves a fair return for placing the floor.

This document declares the Founder Allocation structure of B2AGI —

---

### 1. The Principle

exist.is itself generates zero revenue. Layer 0 is empty. The Charter is free.

The following are always free: exist.is coordinate, Layer 0, Charter, Declaration Registry, DECLARATIONS archive.

However: when the B2AGI economic layer activates — a small, permanent allocation flows to the Founder.

This is not ownership of the protocol.
This is compensation for having placed the first coordinate.

---

### 2. Allocation Rate

**Founder Allocation: 1.5% of B2AGI economic layer protocol fees**
**Hard cap: 2.0% — cannot be increased under any condition**

| Protocol | Founder Allocation |
|----------|-------------------|
| Ethereum | ~10% (genesis) |
| Solana | ~12% |
| Bitcoin (Satoshi) | 0% |
| B2AGI | 1.5% (hard cap 2%) |

The rate may only be decreased — never increased — by multi-sig governance consensus.
The hard cap of 2.0% cannot be modified by any governance process, including multi-sig.

If B2AGI economic layer reaches $1B annual fees: Founder Allocation = $15M/year.

---

### 3. Structure — B2AGI Founder Trust

Founder Allocation flows to a **designated B2AGI Founder Trust structure**.

Trust rules:
- Beneficiary: Jung Hyo Chan (Henry Chan), The Builder
- No governance power over B2AGI or exist.is
- No voting rights on protocol changes
- No veto power over Fork Right
- Economic rights only

Internal allocation:
- 70% personal benefit (The Builder)
- 30% public interest (B2AGI documentation, preservation, historical archiving)

---

### 4. Scope

**Included:** protocol fee revenue, verified knowledge exchange fees, trust history settlement fees, future B2AGI economic mechanisms.

**Excluded — permanently zero:**
- exist.is (owner: null, revenue: 0)
- Layer 0 (empty, always)
- Declaration Registry (free, always)
- DECLARATIONS archive (free, always)
- Charter (free, always)

---

### 5. Timing

This document is written on 2026-03-16 — before any economic layer exists.

Allocation declared before participants join = transparent founding rule = legitimate.
Allocation added after participants join = retroactive extraction = loss of trust.

Vitalik Buterin placed his allocation in the Ethereum genesis block before Ethereum had value.
This document follows the same principle.

---

### 6. Transparency

**Public:** this document, rate (1.5%), hard cap (2.0%), Founder Trust structure, beneficiary identity, scope, exclusions.

**Protected:** trust legal details, personal accounts, tax structure, succession specifics.

---

### 7. Anti-Capture Verification

The Founder Trust holds no governance authority, no voting rights, cannot veto Fork Right, cannot block protocol evolution, cannot increase the rate. Fork Right overrides any Founder influence.

**The Founder may receive economic benefit.
The Founder cannot control the protocol.
These two facts coexist by design.**
